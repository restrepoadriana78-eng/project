Biologist (MSc) with 15+ years of applied research and project coordination in Colombian Andean and Amazonian-piedmont landscapes. Expertise in biodiversity baselines and monitoring, socio-ecological planning, nature-related risk assessment under the TNFD framework and responsible supply-chain approaches. Experienced in designing data-collection strategies, analysing ecological and land-use data, and translating evidence into decisions for corporate, public-sector and territorial stakeholders. Lifelong resident of a coffee-growing landscape, with direct understanding of smallholder coffee systems.
